# Local playback history

Luma 1.0.11 adds an optional Android media-session source for YouTube TV, TizenTube, Prime Video TV and Spotify TV/mobile. It records newly reported playback on this projector, not the services' account-wide histories. Existing Android TV Watch Next, Stremio channel, Trakt and manual entries remain available.

Enable **Luma playback history** in Android notification access, reachable from Launcher settings → Continue Watching → YouTube, Prime & Spotify playback. The notification listener is used to obtain Android media-session access. Luma does not read notification messages or actions. Revoking access stops recording and hides this source's cards. Turn off Continue Watching to pause recording, or clear the current profile's local playback history in that settings page.

Some Android TV firmware, including the tested P6, does not expose a Notification access settings screen. Luma reports this instead of opening unrelated settings. Playback history stays off until the user enables the permission through a supported device method; the rest of the launcher continues to work.

Only the named media packages are observed. Titles, artist text, reported position/duration and artwork are stored in private, backup-disabled app storage, scoped to the active Luma profile when captured. History is bounded to three titles per video app and one Spotify listening item. Actual provider account/profile separation remains the responsibility of each streaming app. Paused entries are not repeatedly made newer; playing positions are sampled every 15 seconds. No playback progress is fabricated when the app reports no position or duration.

Press Up from a supported Home dock tile to view its available titles. Spotify has a separate **Continue Listening** presentation with square album art, song and artist, a subtle progress line and small Liquid Glass controls. Its background gains a restrained ambient tint sampled from the current album cover; the original wallpaper returns on exit. Play/Pause and Next appear only for a matching active Spotify session that reports those actions; otherwise the primary action opens Spotify. A live matching media session can supply its own resume activity and playback action. Otherwise, a provider-matched media link is used if available; with neither, Luma opens the app and explains that title-level opening is unavailable. Exact seeking, previous sessions after process death and DRM provider metadata depend on each app. Ads and streams without useful media metadata cannot supply reliable resume cards.

Artwork delivered as a bitmap is copied into a bounded private cache. Only provider-specific HTTPS artwork domains are accepted otherwise. Persisted media links are checked against their provider and launched explicitly into that package. No app-private databases, account tokens or Accessibility screen scraping are used.

Prime limitation verified on this P6: while paused inside a video, Prime reports a position but null metadata and no session launch intent. Its TV preview channel contains recommendations with no playback positions. Luma therefore records only a generic **Continue in Prime Video** card after actual playback, with no invented title, artwork or percentage. Selecting it opens Prime; reopening the exact title or seeking to the checkpoint is not guaranteed. Use Prime's own Continue Watching row for title-level resumption. YouTube and Spotify retain their detailed media cards.

Build, release lint and 55 JVM tests passed, including link-provider isolation, reported content-ID links, identity stability and reported-progress handling. The P6 listener binds automatically after opening Luma. A real TizenTube video produced title, artwork and progress; selecting its card reopened the same video around its paused 15-second position. The owner confirmed Spotify resume works. Album artwork capture was corrected to prefer album/art fields over the display icon and refresh late artwork; the actual Blurryface cover was inspected after installation. Prime was checked while paused and after Home: its generic app-opening fallback works, but it supplies no title metadata, artwork, duration or session launch intent. Exact title resumption is not verified or promised.
